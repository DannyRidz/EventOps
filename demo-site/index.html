How EventOps works

The organizer stays in control.

  1. 01

    Describe

    Save a specific update by text or upload a voice recording for a draft transcript.

  2. 02

    Review

    Inspect affected records, room conflicts, volunteer coverage and missing facts.

  3. 03

    Confirm

    Correct the interpretation and explicitly confirm new facts before a proposal is created.

  4. 04

    Approve

    Compare the exact change. Transactional checks reject stale or invalid proposals.

  5. 05

    Briefing

    Read or export the approved revision and inspect its history.

Meta integration

Interpretation with clear boundaries.

Muse Spark interprets confirmed updates and selects among deterministically validated options. Muse Voice Transcribe produces a draft transcript for organizer review.

The models cannot approve an official event plan. Deterministic code checks constraints, and the database checks the full proposal again during approval.

Current prototype status

Local manual review, approval, history and briefing work without a model key. General live AI is disabled, and new model calls are paused.

Full live quality evaluation, human accessibility acceptance, the device matrix and hosted operations checks remain open.
